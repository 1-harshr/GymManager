My Gym

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short: My Gym keeps a gym's records (members, plans, payments and check-ins) so its owner and staff can run the front desk. That data belongs to the gym. We don't sell it, show ads or track you, and the app contains no analytics or advertising code.

My Gym is an app for Android, iOS and the web that gym owners and their staff use to manage their gym. This policy explains what it stores, why, who can see it and how to delete it. "We" means the developer of My Gym; "the gym" means the business using the app.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email address from Google when you sign in with Google, or your phone number if you sign in with one, and which gym you belong to and your role there (owner or staff).
  • The gym's details: its name, address, logo, plans and prices, and settings such as reminder messages.
  • Members' records, entered by the gym: names, phone numbers, photos, notes, memberships, pauses, payments and check-ins.

The camera is used only when you take a member's photo or a gym logo. Notifications are used for reminders such as memberships ending today.

Why

Only to run the app: showing the gym its records on every phone and browser its staff use, working out who has expired or still owes money, and preparing messages to members. We don't use the data for anything else.

Who can see it

  • The gym's owner and staff. Each gym's data is kept apart from every other gym's, and only people the owner has invited can open it.
  • Supabase, the service that hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage, which processes the data on our behalf.
  • Google, when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), for messages to members. By default the app opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message filled in, and you send it yourself. If the gym connects its WhatsApp Business number (directly or through Make.com), those messages, with the member's name and phone number, go out through that service.

The AI insights on the Home screen are worked out on your phone, and nothing from them is sent anywhere.

How it's kept

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is protected by access rules in the database. An encrypted copy of the database is made every night and kept for 30 days, so a gym's records can be recovered after a mistake or failure.

Deleting data

  • Your account: in the app, open Settings → Delete account, or use the web app's delete account page. Deletion is immediate. If you're the gym's only owner and alone there, the whole gym is deleted with it; if others work there, you first make one of them an owner.
  • A member's records: the gym can delete a member at any time. If you're a member and want your records removed or corrected, ask your gym, which controls them, or write to us and we'll pass it on.

Deleted data disappears from nightly backups within 30 days.

Children

My Gym is for gym owners and staff, and isn't meant for anyone under 18 to sign in to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
